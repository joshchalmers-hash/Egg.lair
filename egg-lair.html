Egg Lair

Humans have heard a rumour: eat a dragon egg and live forever. Waves of them are coming for your nest.

Place dragons beside the path. Kill thieves before they carry an egg out. Tap a dropped egg to carry it home.

Fire: heavy hits, splash when grown. Mages ignore it.
Ice: chills and slows. Druids shrug it off.
Venom: weak bite, lingering poison. Priests heal through it.
Pick a dragon, then tap a stone tile beside the path.
